# MUSICTOOL DAW — Sample bank acknowledgements

V7.2 loads recorded instrument samples from the following public-domain (CC0 1.0) sources. Samples are fetched from their upstream repositories and cached locally by the browser for subsequent playback.

## Instruments

- **Meta Open Sound — Meta Piano**: piano recordings, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound
- **FreePats — FSBS Electric Guitar Clean (small SFZ sample set)**: direct-recorded electric guitar samples, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-clean
- **Meta Open Sound — Meta Steel Guitar**: steel-string acoustic guitar recordings, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound
- **Karoryfer Samples — Big Little Bass**: plucked electric bass recordings, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-little-bass

## Drums and percussion

- **Phazite Sounds**: acoustic and electronic drum one-shots, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/august-byrne/phazite-sounds

The upstream repositories publish these sample collections under CC0 1.0. Their sound assets may be used, modified and redistributed, including commercially, subject to the terms of CC0. This acknowledgement is included for provenance; CC0 does not require attribution.

The app's own source code remains under the repository's existing license. These sound assets are separate from that code license.
