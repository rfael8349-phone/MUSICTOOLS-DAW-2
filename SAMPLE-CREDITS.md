# MUSICTOOL DAW — Audio sample sources

The V7.2 instrument bank uses recorded audio samples, not generated oscillator approximations, for the piano, steel-string guitar, bass and drum voices.

All bundled source collections listed below are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. They may be used commercially, modified and redistributed without attribution. This notice is retained for provenance and transparency.

## Piano and steel-string guitar

- Source: [Meta Open Sound](https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound)
- Collections: Meta Piano and Meta Steel Guitar
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- The DAW loads selected OGG preview samples from the collections and caches them locally in Cache Storage after the first successful download.

## Electric bass

- Source: [Karoryfer Samples — Big Little Bass](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-little-bass)
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Selected plucked WAV samples are mapped across the bass keyboard by playback-rate transposition.

## Drum kit and Launchpad

- Source: [Phazite Sounds](https://github.com/august-byrne/phazite-sounds)
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Selected acoustic and electronic drum one-shots are loaded from the pack and cached locally.

## Runtime and offline behavior

The audio files are fetched from their public source repositories on first use / first preload, decoded by the Web Audio API and stored in the browser's Cache Storage. After a successful first download, the browser can reuse the cached samples offline. Clearing website data or browser caches removes those cached instrument files, so they must be downloaded again.

The built-in SYNTH voice remains procedurally generated by design. The PIANO, GUITAR, BASS, DRUM and Launchpad voices use recorded sample sources.
